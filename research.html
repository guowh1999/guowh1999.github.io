BIND — deconvolving missing values in proteomics

Most pipelines impute missing protein values away. BIND instead deconvolves where the missingness comes from, using a kNN core: it recovered biomarkers 85% of conventional methods never find, including GPC3 in a hepatocellular carcinoma cohort.

MultiGraphSCP — single-cell proteomics with graph neural networks

A model that encodes multi-view cell relationships for single-cell proteomics and feeds downstream clustering and spatial applications. On cell clustering it lifted ARI and ASW by 0.38 and 0.45.

  • GNN
  • Single-cell

SpaECM — tumour–CAF gene interactions in space

A toolkit for spatial transcriptomics: cluster cancer-associated fibroblasts by neighbourhood, integrate regions, then test gene co-expression at the tumour–CAF interface. Across 21 datasets it surfaced pan-cancer and cancer-specific interactions.

Urological cancer burden in China, 1990–2021

A GBD analysis of incidence, prevalence, mortality and DALY across China and 34 provinces, with AI-based forecasts by age, sex and geography, and scenario work on smoking, high BMI and glucose control.
